Purpose
The R6 guided-x0 controller opened at zero-based index6, then stopped after one
aligned endpoint and did not repair the final result. This R9 tests a cheap
ordinary stop-confirmation rule before crediting a more expensive native-first
controller. MotionEcho, Adaptive Guidance and PAG/CAG are reviewed in the frozen
R2 query_time_posterior_gate_review.txt section6; this baseline makes no novelty
claim. A result on this known failure case is not independent validation.

decision but cannot mutate actual state or streak. First observation5 can only
control forward6. All50 blocks in one forward share the latched decision.
