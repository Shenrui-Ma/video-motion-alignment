Actual rule
The original scanner already computes both delayed and advanced proposals. It requires reliable lags at confidence 0.3 and 0.5, agreement within two frames, then per-null common-support comparisons against all five lags -2..2. Every comparison retains coverage >=0.8, at least eight valid physical frames, positive weight, shifted residual <=0.5, and gain strictly greater than 0.5.
The signed wrapper admits advanced_candidate in addition to delayed_candidate; both lags must be below -2 or above +2 consistently. It delegates grouping and representative ordering to the unchanged policy. A mixed-sign temporal-overlap component remains one component; the member with strongest absolute cost benefit is its representative. This ranking is absolute benefit, not normalized min_gain. A stronger opposite-sign member can suppress another candidate and can change representative sign between observations.
Persistence requires same representative direction, shared arm, and overlap >=0.5 of the shorter interval, at consecutive denoising indices beginning at zero. Empty/UNKNOWN observations clear previous representatives. A sign flip is not persistence. This correctly implements the frozen scope; it is not a direction-separated grouping algorithm. The mixed-component and sign-flip tests cover these intended semantics.
