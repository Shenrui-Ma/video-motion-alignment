Known competing explanations
Mass route beating hard mask can simply compensate hard mask's lower total reference mass. Hard mask has m'=m*c/(1-m+m*c), where c is same-time conditional reference mass. Frame alignment is already present in Vidu S2 and Wan-Animate-2; DIAL already strengthens reference logits. Mass normalization is itself expressible as a query-dependent logit bias. These statements prevent claiming novelty from notation or a port alone.

Phase-control amendment before results
Reading future reference may compensate existing late output; +4 is therefore a signed phase perturbation, not a control guaranteed to fail. If both +0 and +4 improve, add -4 with equal strength and compare signed phase response. A shifted control improving by itself does not falsify temporal correspondence; identical effects across offsets weaken that explanation.
