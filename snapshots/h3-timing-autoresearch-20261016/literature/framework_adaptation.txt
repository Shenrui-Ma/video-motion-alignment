Necessary framework borrowing for this session

ScientistTwo: subset-first empirical filtering, independent ablation review, and auditing claims against runnable code and actual outputs. A failure or a changed baseline sends the task back to validation rather than to manuscript polishing. Source: https://scientist-two.github.io/ and https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19644 . Its automated review acceptance is not actual conference acceptance or a guarantee for this project.

IdeaScientist: distinguish same-problem prior-art retrieval from cross-setting mechanism transfer, keep a gap statement and a falsifiable intervention separate, and challenge rediscovery/relabeling against the closest methods. Source: https://github.com/jiarui-liu/IdeaScientist/blob/main/README.md and https://github.com/jiarui-liu/IdeaScientist/blob/main/ideascientist/evaluation/prompts.py . We did not train its RL agents, install its full retrieval corpus, or run its benchmark.

Practical realization here: three independent agents handle prior-art review, implementation/provenance review, and motion measurement; the root agent controls the only model-inference queue, integrates criticism, and makes promotion/rejection decisions. Decisions and failed attempts remain in the research ledger.
