# Narrow R4 review before outcomes

Both proposed jobs are executable, useful diagnostics. Neither requires a new general literature category to be considered worth testing. Their utility, novelty and future combination value should be judged separately.

**Passive Y/C capture.** Save Y across the42 target-video bins and C=Y+the existing full same-bin correction on the six event bins in the same forward, layer, head basis, spatial layout and normalization. Do not substitute raw reference values for C. The fixed heads0/14/28/42, block25 and calls6/10/14 are legitimate preselected probes; do not choose a successful head after seeing results. The passive native run must replay the existing .41 native's actual inputs/noise, common saved tensors and decoded RGB. A failure of that gate means the observer changed the experimental baseline, not that a new mechanism worked.

C is an existing actuator's counterfactual target, not ground-truth correct motion. A closer target-video feature at another time is evidence about representability of that correction, not proof of phase correction or action preservation. Large shared Y content can make raw cosine similarity look uniformly excellent. A useful predeclared residual check is

`||Y(t') − C(t)|| / ||Y(t) − C(t)||`,

equivalently comparing `Y(t')−Y(t)` to `C(t)−Y(t)`. Keep the unmodified t'=t candidate as the ratio1 control and abstain when the correction denominator is near zero. Preserve full candidate curves and do not report only their minimum. Freeze the candidate physical-time range and exact bin-center mapping; bin offsets are not uniformly4 frames. If a retrieval or fitting procedure is developed after this probe, declare it a new diagnostic rather than retroactively claiming preregistration. Synthetic C=Y(t+known shift), flat sequences and no-correction C=Y(t) are cheap model-free checks.

**Middle-ten-block full correction.** Blocks20–29, calls6–14 and otherwise identical science inputs form a reasonable practical depth/compute ablation against the same .41 full50-block result. Use the original four timing windows, confidence thresholds, common-support policy and fixed55/86 visual checks. Report local-event benefit, off-window changes, edit-quality limits and actual time. A reduction450→90 correction invocations is80% fewer correction invocations, not80% less total diffusion compute or80% faster video generation.

Separate total graph wall time, sampler wall time and passive capture/I/O overhead. Existing full50 has no new Y/C disk writes; either disable that recorder in the intervention timing job or explicitly report its unmatched overhead. If the passive native changes trajectory, use a matched new-runtime full50 instead of borrowing the old one. The current experiment can identify usefulness of this specific ten-block subset; it cannot isolate middle-depth specificity from fewer updates or prove this layer range optimal. Equal-budget early/late subsets are a future mechanism control if that stronger claim becomes relevant, not a prerequisite for this minimal test.

This review adds no inference, changes no main metric, and does not prescribe a new online threshold. A successful efficiency/quality result can remain useful even if the routing idea has precedents, provided the comparison and attribution are explicit.
