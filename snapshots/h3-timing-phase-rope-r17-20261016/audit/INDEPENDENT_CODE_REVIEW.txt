3. Group probability mass and reference scope
---------------------------------------------
At a fixed layer input, let m_ref be the original full-attention probability
mass assigned to reference-video keys. The formula
 O' = O + alpha*m_ref*(O_ref(rotatedQ)-O_ref(originalQ))
corresponds to replacing only that group's probabilities by the convex mixture
(1-alpha)*P_ref + alpha*m_ref*softmax(rotatedQ K_ref^T). Its sum remains m_ref;
other groups' original probabilities remain unchanged. The reference identity
image is outside the selected video segment. The main canvas query interval is
checked disjoint from video reference keys. Clamping tiny positive logmass to0
is inherited numerical protection, not an exact global probability claim under
finite precision. Audits expose excess and finite statistics. Future states,
future group masses and final image pixels need not be invariant.
