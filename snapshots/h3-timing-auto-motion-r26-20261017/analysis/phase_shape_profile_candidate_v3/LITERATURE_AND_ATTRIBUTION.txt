Primary sources checked before v3 execution, 2026-10-17

1. Zhou & de la Torre, Canonical Time Warping for Alignment of Human Behavior, NeurIPS 2009. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2009/file/2ca65f58e35d9ad45bf7f3ae5cfd08f1-Paper.pdf
Read section 2.2: DTW constrains a temporal correspondence path by boundaries, continuity and monotonicity, solved with DP. Section 3 combines CCA feature transforms with DTW, and section 4 adds local spatial transforms. Thus temporal order and nuisance-adjusted alignment have clear prior art. Our sparse interval chain does not implement CTW's learned CCA projection or its full correspondence path.

2. Su & Hua, Order-Preserving Wasserstein Distance for Sequence Matching, CVPR 2017. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2017/html/Su_Order-Preserving_Wasserstein_Distance_CVPR_2017_paper.html
Official abstract retrieved through search; direct page/PDF fetch returned 403. It uses inverse-difference-moment and KL-to-temporal-prior regularizers in OT, optimized by matrix scaling. This is soft temporal regularization, not the hard all-phase interval edge of v3. The broad claim of preserving order is already established. No uninspected theorem is attributed.

3. Liu et al., Learning to Align Sequential Actions in the Wild (VAVA), CVPR 2022. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Liu_Learning_To_Align_Sequential_Actions_in_the_Wild_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf
Official indexed full text section 3.2/3.3 retrieved: consistency and optimality priors form a mixture; virtual frames absorb unmatched/background frames. It learns sequential representations and supports nonmonotonic order. V3 instead assumes the edit is intended to preserve action order. VAVA demonstrates why this assumption is task-specific and cannot be imposed on arbitrary pairs or user-requested action reordering.

Attribution: SO(2) profiling, set-valued decisions, DTW monotonicity and max-weight chaining are mature mathematics. This finite test evaluates their combination with the already frozen observer. It establishes neither a new theory nor a publication-level contribution.
