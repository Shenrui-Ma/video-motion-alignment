NEXT MECHANISM DECISION — conditional development recommendation
2026-10-17; read-only assessment; no inference, runtime, threshold or queue change.

Recommendation
Finish the current R27 outcome evaluation and the already-defined R30 forward+reverseBLOCK experiment. If the current automatic global-spatial Full executor gives useful timing correction but visible collateral changes, the next mechanism to test is a CURRENT-TARGET + SOURCE joint spatial support for that same temporal executor. This is one specific hypothesis about where the correction must act. Do not label a dynamic mask, union operation, or automation itself a new architecture.

Why this candidate has an evidential foothold
R27 already chooses physical-time windows and denoising indices from the current Native endpoint, but applies its Full correction to every spatial query in the selected temporal bins. R16 supplies an unusually concrete failure diagnostic: its source-only forearm ROI retained the native lag (direction 13/13, wrist 12/12), whereas global Full was 1/1. Source forearm points had alpha 1, while visible delayed target forearms had final mean alpha 0.166/0.067. More importantly, the exactly bound preintervention Native index5 left forearm had mean SOFT MASK WEIGHT alpha 0.158 across 21 sampled points at confidence .3 (16 valid frames), and .194 at .5 (13 valid). The positive-weight point fraction at .3 was .2113; .158 is not a coverage fraction. The right arm was largely UNKNOWN at that early point. Thus the desired reference location can miss the current delayed target location. This is a testable support mismatch, not an established cause of failure: clean-endpoint pose does not identify a noisy query's feature contents, and attention propagates spatially.

Proposed narrow structural change
Keep the temporal route, attention-group mass, layer range and alpha exactly as in the validated Full operator. For a selected arm and physical interval W, derive the target arm region from the CURRENT pre-intervention Native endpoint, and the desired arm region from the source. Use their union, mapped through the actual frame/latent/query geometry, as a binary or already-frozen soft spatial query support M_W. Then O_new(q) = O_native(q) + M_W(q) * delta_Full(q). No new reference-value decomposition or attention marginal projection is proposed. Source and target coordinates share the image canvas, but identity-dependent body proportions and occlusion remain explicit limitations. If the target region is not observable, this variant returns UNKNOWN rather than silently inventing a location. Untouched rows must be bitwise native within that attention call; this does not promise unchanged pixels after later layers.

The necessary minimal attribution test
First use already archived Native decodes/poses to quantify target/source union positive-weight fractions, mean soft mask weights, missingness and support size at eligible pre-intervention states. This is an offline feasibility check, not evidence of efficacy, and must include the known normal development seed. Do not select a new successful frame or alter thresholds from final videos.
If that check supports a real support difference, freeze one development schedule and the existing physical window for a small COMPONENT experiment. The global Full executor is the positive control. Compare the joint union with a source-only expansion having exactly the same number of query rows per temporal bin, deterministic nearest-source expansion/tie-breaking, and the same alpha, layers and sampling indices. The original R16 ROI is a historical failure reference, not an adequate size-matched control. Both local arms must compute their support from their own current Native endpoint before any intervention; no future pose or old diverged trajectory is admissible. If expanded source support already covers the target equally well, record that the proposed comparison is non-identifying rather than tune another mask to win.
Measure actual reference-mass-weighted correction magnitude as a diagnostic: equal row count and alpha do not imply equal correction energy. This experiment can test placement versus simple spatial expansion; it cannot isolate all possible dose effects. Freeze the schedule initially so a changed feedback decision is not mistaken for a better spatial operator. Only after a component benefit is established should the unchanged R27 selector drive this executor online.
The efficacy target is timing no worse than global Full on the original four windows and a reliably visible reduction of collateral deformation/ghosting in the fixed 55/86/105 views and 42–82 clip. Use all 186 full-time scan rows, direction and wrist at both frozen confidence thresholds, common valid support and UNKNOWN retention. If global Full does not show discernible collateral damage, this case cannot establish the proposed quality benefit. If union and equal-size expansion behave alike, the evidence favors ordinary coverage expansion rather than a joint-support mechanism. If both local variants fail, global/contextual query support may be needed; do not keep increasing masks post hoc.

Why the other two candidates are not the selected next new intervention
Forward Full + reverseBLOCK is a justified missing factorial cell and is already R30. Existing N/F/B results motivate testing its interaction, but reverse-only partial improvements do not predict that FB reduces ghosting. Complete its exact-component and final quality gates first; if FB provides the desired benefit, prioritize validating that result before adding another component. OmniTransfer, Identity as Presence, FullDiT2 and Vidu already establish closely related asymmetric reference/target designs. R30 is a bounded causal interaction test, not a claim that asymmetry was invented here.
Same-state counterfactual acceptance is lower priority. Current-Native versus current-Full endpoint scoring can be useful, but R13 shows that an improved guided endpoint and its next native continuation are different objects: 6→7 internal lag was +6 after Full-update versus +10 after Native-update, 10→11 +1 versus +2, and 14→15 0 versus 0. These are three forks of one trajectory and not independent decoded-pose or final-video efficacy. R12 also demonstrates observer residual error. An endpoint-only accept rule may select a temporary prediction improvement or reject small cumulative benefits; stronger lookahead costs extra NFE and is already related to derivative-free candidate-selection guidance. Generic candidate scoring/acceptance is established by SVDD/CoDe and related work. It presently has a weaker attribution route than the measured spatial-support mismatch.

Existing results and prior-art boundary
R3 DC and SP were not universally ineffective: R3 DC matched Full timing on the primary window; SP was less accurate and neither demonstrated a quality advantage. R2 DC differs from R3 in actual encoded inputs, so do not infer a hardware cause or pool them as one replication. The proposed union does not recycle DC temporal residual subtraction or SP spatial-marginal preservation.
Motion by Queries (arXiv:2412.07750v3, Appendix B.10/B.11, independently being checked by the literature owner) already uses per-step clean-estimate masks, same-frame cross-shot KV and query-flow transport. Related local feature/motion guidance also exists. A defensible contribution would have to establish why CURRENT erroneous support plus DESIRED support, with a size-controlled comparison and observable abstention, improves the timing/quality trade-off in this editing setting. Merely adding target segmentation to an existing mask is not sufficient novelty evidence. This note proposes a falsifiable component investigation, not a publication claim.
