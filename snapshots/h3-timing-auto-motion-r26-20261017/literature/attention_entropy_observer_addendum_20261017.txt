Attention-based observation: additional primary-source check
Date: 2026-10-17
Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2504.12027v2
Title: Understanding Attention Mechanism in Video Diffusion Models
Sections checked: 3.1, 3.4, 4.1–4.2, Appendix7.4.

IE-Adapt studies identity/uniform attention perturbations. Its generation method selects a maximum-entropy layer and forms additional guidance from perturbed predictions. For editing, it ranks layers by attention entropy and replaces the lower-half maps with source maps; real-video sources require inversion. Layer choice from internal attention statistics therefore already has direct precedent. This source does not establish that H3 reference-attention time offsets estimate physical action delay.

R46 implication: keep the preregistered single layer, four heads and two restored states. Readout entropy is descriptive. No entropy-trigger novelty claim, adaptive head selection, or threshold tuning from these two states. Examine reference mass, temporal distribution, and value-readout difference together. A low off-diagonal mass or similar values can make a temporal-map discrepancy irrelevant. Conversely, a clear difference between two seeds only motivates independent validation; it does not prove a detector or causal correction.

This is a read-only literature addendum. R46 numerical protocol remains unchanged. Existing STR-Match/Motion4Motion/Motion by Queries checks still apply.
