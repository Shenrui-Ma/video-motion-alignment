Control evidence vs exact phase estimation
A possible follow-up would retain a set of admissible delays and trigger only when it lies wholly outside [-2,2] on one side. A wide set such as [7,15] could support intervention without identifying delay to two frames. This changes the decision target, so it requires a separate frozen protocol and synthetic failures, not post-hoc relaxation of v1. Tolerance remains an engineering bound; no coverage probability or robustness guarantee has been established. Constant-rotation profiling can leave delay unidentifiable; arbitrary time-varying shape changes remain confounds.

Prior art for mathematical ingredients
Bernabeu et al., On Time-Delay Estimation Accuracy Limit Under Phase Uncertainty, primary paper https://www.tesa.prd.fr/documents/26/on_time-delay_estimation_accuracy_limit_under_phase_uncertainty.pdf. Its signal model includes phase as a nuisance with uncertain sensor information. Equations 8-17 combine Fisher information and block-matrix elimination to derive the time-delay/Doppler bound. Useful evidence that delay/phase coupling and nuisance elimination are established estimation theory; its Gaussian bandlimited radio signal assumptions do not transfer as guarantees to pose directions.
Risso et al., Set-Valued Model Predictive Control, CDC2021, DOI10.1109/CDC45484.2021.9682993. Author primary https://hybrid.soe.ucsc.edu/node/2402 and https://hybrid.soe.ucsc.edu/sites/default/files/preprints/253.pdf. Search-indexed author manuscript describes set-valued states/maps and costs over set trajectories. Direct PDF fetch timed out; full theorem assumptions not audited. Set-based uncertainty-aware control itself is existing work. No novelty claim for that general idea.

Method section4 identifies temporal heads, builds semantic-refined cross-frame displacement maps, optimizes latent motion loss, and injects reference value features through low-entropy structural heads. Eq4 adds correspondence-based bias to cross-frame attention. Main CogVideoX experiment uses50 denoising steps and fixed12 optimization steps; official config guidance range[50,38],5 optimization steps per guided step. Wan appendix exists. Thus selecting motion heads, semantic logit bias, reference value injection, and fixed-range displacement guidance already have close prior art. Our current same-time mass-preserving H3 reference routing plus feedback physical-window choice differs operationally, but automatic profiling evidence and final quality need actual validation. Do not claim head selection or a correspondence bias as independently new. This was a targeted check, not an exhaustive novelty certificate.
