PhaseLock (arXiv2606.06361v1; NVIDIA page labels ICML2026): frozen-model 2-step sampling supplies inter-frame latent differences, then a second same-noise full trajectory receives the difference residual in later frame latents. Its schedule is a configured interval with linear strength decay. Paper sections4.2–4.3 and Algorithm1 read. Fourier phase sensitivity and preserving a few-step motion prior are existing ideas. Our source-referenced physical action-lag detector uses a different signal and attention intervention; novelty still requires positive causal controls. Fourier phase is not itself measured physical event delay.

MotionFlow (arXiv2412.05275v1; AAAI2026 official landing): ZeroScope DDIM inversion extracts subject-token cross-attention masks using a relative maximum threshold. Cross/self/temporal attention losses optimize noisy latents at inference. Sections3.1–3.3 read. Attention-derived motion guidance and adaptive spatial masks are existing. It does not establish our particular per-action lag feedback or automatic evidence-gated start/stop. Repository code was not audited here.

These readings add prior-art boundaries; they do not prove our method novel or effective. HALO already has a separate detailed local review and was not duplicated.
