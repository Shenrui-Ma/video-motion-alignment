# Frozen source ROI: what the existing poses do and do not show

This is a post-outcome diagnostic, not a changed primary metric or a new mask-selection rule. The frozen mask and all model outputs remain unchanged.

At physical frames 50–65, source forearm points lie in ROI coefficient 1.0. The final uncorrected native and ROI-only arms largely lie outside that same source-derived region. Using 21 evenly spaced forearm points mapped to their actual 32-pixel query patch, the ROI output has mean alpha 0.166 (left) and 0.067 (right), with positive-mask fractions 25.3% and 10.4% at confidence 0.3. Both arms have 16 valid frames at this threshold. The detailed 0.5 missing-data denominators are retained in final_geometry_mask_coverage.json. The successful Full output overlaps almost entirely, but that is a consequence-compatible final geometry and cannot establish the failure cause.

A preintervention check can reuse the already decoded native index5 endpoint exactly. Its video and audio raw signatures, sigma, seed, geometry and normalization match R16’s actual index5 capture; the old decode is complete and its output MP4 SHA matches the saved pose. The full binding is in prefix005_geometry_mask_coverage.json. No new model, VAE or pose run was performed. Its left-arm mean alpha is 0.158 at confidence0.3 (16/16 valid) and0.194 at0.5 (13/16 valid). The right arm has only4/16 valid frames at0.3 and none at0.5; a reliable bilateral preintervention coverage conclusion is unavailable.

These observations make spatial mismatch a concrete hypothesis: a mask tied to the desired reference location does not cover much of the visible delayed arm in the clean endpoint estimate. They do not prove that the actual noisy sampler input or the contextual query features at indices6–14 are outside the effective intervention footprint. A spatial patch is not an isolated anatomical feature, and later blocks/updates can propagate changes. Decode/pose uncertainty and changed body proportions also limit the proxy.

Do not enlarge the source mask, relax confidence, move the selected window, or treat the final Full overlap as a counterfactual causal demonstration based on this result. The uniform equal-alpha-sum control is still needed to interpret the tested distribution choice. Even with that result, equal alpha sum is not equal attention-mass-weighted correction dose, and an effective global Full operator does not imply that the same correction restricted to desired forearm coordinates is sufficient.
