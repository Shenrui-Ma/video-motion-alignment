- R55 actual conditioning audit/router components. Their data paths are bound by a small standalone contract accepting only this probe's exact graph and audit directory. This contract is confined to the fresh run's scripts directory.
- Original compatibility bootstrap/INT8 row chunking/backend audit bytes, copied into the run. Original flags plus --reserve-vram8. No alternative attention kernel, checkpoint, dtype, LoRA or sampler is introduced.
- Official first sampler input is archived as audit/first_input_raw.pt. Initial packed noise and clean conditioning must match R54 records.
- Native forward, then all27bin Full on the identical input/sigma/condition. Full uses the existing mass-preserve attention and actual QueryGain all-one path. Native output remains live during Full. Both branches must execute50blocks; routed counts0/50.
- Native normalized video/audio endpoints must equal actual R54 step000 tensors and signatures. Full output is checked finite. The complete sampler grid remains present throughout; after two predictions, CapacityProbeComplete intentionally stops graph execution before Euler updates/final decoding.

Interpretation
- before_first_forward lifetime_max_* retains allocator peaks from preprocessing/model loading before our first reset.
- before/after allocated_bytes is live Torch tensor allocation; reserved_bytes includes allocator cache. reserved_minus_allocated_bytes quantifies that difference. driver_free_bytes is whole-device free memory at those boundaries and includes unrelated users' activity.
- loaded_weight_bytes/model_weight_bytes/lowvram_patch_count are the actual model patcher's residency accounting. They describe model weights, not all tensor memory.
- Each branch synchronizes, samples entry, resets peak counters, invokes exactly one model prediction, synchronizes and records peaks before CPU finite/equality checks. peak_allocated_above_entry_bytes describes additional live allocation during that call. peak_reserved_above_entry_bytes describes additional allocator reservation. Their meanings differ from adding an isolated routing-only bound to old NVML usage.
- These are two first-state forwards with the largest actual sequence. They establish measured reserve8 Native/Full behavior at that state; they are not a completed20step or all-case capacity proof. Keep a root-chosen explicit safety margin and monitor the first formal run. No margin or freed-memory amount is invented here.
- A certificate must separate preprocessing/lifetime allocator peak, Native/Full call peaks, own-PID sampled NVML usage, model residency and safety margin. If Native equality fails or Full OOMs, do not mark a capacity certificate passed.
- Successful measurement is a controlled graph error followed by exact service-group cleanup, with inference_complete=false. The runner never calls it a successful generated video. Its lifetime watcher stops the service if the exact controller generation exits. Other users' processes are never signalled.
