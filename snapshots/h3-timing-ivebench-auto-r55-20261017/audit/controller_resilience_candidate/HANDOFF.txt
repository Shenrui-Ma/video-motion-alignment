Files changed inside overlay:
1. scripts/job.py
   - NVIDIA process-query TimeoutExpired/CalledProcessError become the existing typed ResourceUnavailable.
   - wait_host_admission catches that type, replacing obsolete message-string matching.
   - Each attempt checks the exact service generation; success checks it again.
   - Resource deadline is positive and at most600seconds. No successful admission is accepted after the deadline. The existing NVIDIA/queue calls each have finite10second timeouts, so an in-flight call may finish after the deadline before failure is returned.
   - Retry records attempt, elapsed time, reason and resource snapshot. Identity/data/graph/queue/HTTP errors keep their original hard failure path.
2. scripts/service.py
   - A PID that disappears between uid and full identity reads becomes ResourceUnavailable. A live reused/unregistered own identity still raises immediately.

   - Own GPU cap, permitted identities, command, precision, reserve-vram and cleanup implementation are unchanged.
3. scripts/run_queue.py
   - Noncritical NVIDIA telemetry timeout/nonzero exit records sample:null/sample_available:false and preserves the running job.
