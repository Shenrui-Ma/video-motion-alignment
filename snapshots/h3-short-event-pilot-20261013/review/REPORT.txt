Other MP4 files (adaptive controls, prepared-source / VAE diagnostics) are listed separately.
No model scores are created. Original inputs and outputs are never overwritten.

Derivatives:
- assets/: full decoded frame contact sheets, 30 frames per page, zero-based frame IDs.
- derived/: SOURCE/A/B comparison at 24 FPS; same-frame-count quarter-speed at 6 FPS.
- derived/*_event/: every frame in annotated event window plus six-frame margins.
- manifest.json: exact files, hashes, frame counts, warnings, and rendering failures.
Missing formal results appear as PENDING. A frame-count mismatch is conspicuous; comparison
uses the longest sequence and labeled black panels for missing frames rather than trimming
or repeating the last frame. FPS mismatches prevent misleading comparison rendering.
